## Notes

- All estimates assume standard 8-hour workdays
- High poly creates the detailed reference model
- Low poly is the optimized in-game mesh
- UVs are the texture coordinate layout for the model
- Bake transfers high poly detail to low poly model
- Textures include all material maps (diffuse, normal, roughness, etc.)
