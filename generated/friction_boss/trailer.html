The Shift

A producer's Monday morning.

Same producer. Same headcount. Different day. This is the most concrete way to feel what we're building toward.

Today
~60% maintenance / 40% strategy
  • Catch up on weekend Slack
  • Chase status from leads
  • Update sprint plans by hand
  • Draft tickets, prep standups
  • Notice a Battle dependency at risk → ping → wait
  • By 11am, no strategic work has happened
→
Steady State
~80% strategy / 20% maintenance
  • Read agent-prepared briefing in 5 minutes
  • What shipped over the weekend, what's at risk
  • What decisions need you today
  • What your ICs need clarity on
  • Spend hour 1 on the single highest-leverage thing
  • Strategic work happening by 9:30
